King Bible Chain Reference: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

King Bible Chain Reference is made by 72 After (72after.dev). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Your notes and highlights stay on your phone. Everything you create in the app, including notes, highlights and verse connections, is saved only on your device. We never receive or see it, and the app doesn't back it up or copy it anywhere else. If you uninstall the app, that data is deleted.

We don't collect personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and we don't sell or share any personal information.

No ads, analytics or tracking. The app has no advertising and no analytics, and it contains no third-party tracking tools.

Bible text comes from the internet. To show scripture, the app requests Bible passages from the free Bible API at bible.helloao.org. Those requests include only the passage and translation you're reading, not your notes or any personal information. Like any website, that service may receive your device's IP address as part of the connection.

Children. The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes. We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact. For privacy questions, email 72afterd8@gmail.com.
